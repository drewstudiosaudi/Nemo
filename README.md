## Proudly Made in Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦

Nemo is a high-performance programming language designed with security and speed at its core.

### Key Features
* **Offline-First:** Nemo operates entirely on your local device. There are no central servers, ensuring complete privacy and independence.
* **Lightning Fast:** By eliminating server dependencies, Nemo provides ultra-fast response times for all your development needs.
* **Enhanced Security:** Your files are encrypted to prevent unauthorized access. We leverage advanced AI integration to monitor and protect the integrity of your local project files.
* **Privacy Focused:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your data. Your code stays on your machine.
